# cuayo

## What is Cuayo?

The world runs on **dopamine**.

People chase it through various ways, which include:

- Spending money (a.k.a "Flexing")

- Winning competitions

- Self-improvement

- and much more...

But while spending can feel good in the moment, it often leads to impulse purchases, poor budgeting, and regret.

So we asked a simple question: **What if people could get dopamine by SAVING money instead of spending it?**

**Cuayo** is our answer.

## The Idea

Cuayo turns saving money into a **competitive game**.

Instead of flexing how much you spend, you compete on how much you save.
Instead of impulse buys, you get motivation, rankings, and insights.

With Cuayo, saving isn't just a boring thing that is necessary for your life and hard to do. Saving becomes something you can:

1. Compete on

2. Track over time

3. Improve with data-driven feedback

You do not need much effort to start Cuayo. All you need is 3 steps that take less than a couple of minutes:

1. Sign up and create an account

2. Add your credit cards on the Profile page

3. Live your life as usual! Come back to Cuayo whenever you want to see how you perform!

Cuayo will automatically **record** your transactions, **analyze** your spending behavior, and **update** your rankings in real time

## Features

🥇 **Rankings**

See how you rank against other users from all around the world!

You could filter the leaderboard by:

- Time frame (daily, weekly, monthly)

- Expense category

- User groups

Rankings are also shown in percentiles, and you can check the leaderboard to see who's at the top and who's around you.

📈 **Analytics**

See visual summaries of your spending patterns! We offer a chart of the distribution of your spendings based on category, as well as a budget checker to see if you have gone over/under the budget.

Personalized insights based on your historical data! Cuayo gives simple actionable suggestions to improve future financial decisions

Need more detail? Click “Generate Detailed Report” to receive an in-depth, AI-generated spending plan

🕒 **History**

Track your ranking history over time!

View a complete list of your recent transactions

See how your financial habits evolve

🕶️ **Privacy First**

Enable Anonymous Mode in your profile!

Your name is hidden on leaderboards while still allowing you to compete
